    How this works

    • Type any name. FaceBlock gathers public reference photos of that person (from Wikipedia, Wikidata, and Wikimedia Commons), sends only the name you typed, and keeps everything else on this device.
    • Matching runs entirely on this device. Images, face data, and your browsing are never uploaded anywhere.
    • Before a new person is blocked, FaceBlock shows you the gathered faces so you can confirm them — automatically collected photos can occasionally include the wrong person.
    • This is a research preview. It scans ordinary webpage images only — faces that are tiny, obscured, or inside videos/canvases can be missed. It does not guarantee 100% blocking.
