Experimental, uncalibrated starting point — not the 0.73 default, which was tuned on a synthetic benchmark, not real photos. Lower masks more eagerly (more false positives); higher is stricter. A face is masked only when at least min(5, identity's reference count) of its embeddings clear the threshold — changing the slider re-evaluates instantly, no re-inference.
